Gear position
Drives the motor closed-loop to that gear's angle setpoint; sending F/R/S/B cancels an in-progress move.
Gear angles built-in defaults – not read from the board
Setpoints in degrees from the taught zero: whole numbers, 0–300°, rising from G1 to G16 at least 5° apart. Save sends one An=deg per changed gear (in an order the board accepts at every step); the board keeps the table in flash, so it survives power cycles and firmware updates. A move already running keeps its old target. G16 also sets the boot-time wrap point (the middle of the arc beyond G16): at power-off keep the shaft within (360−G16)/2 degrees below zero or above G16.
Zero calibration
Jog to the reference position first (Drive / Stop / Brake), then teach: the shaft's current angle becomes 0° and its raw encoder angle is saved to flash, so gear positions survive power cycles. Ignored while a gear move is running. The Zero cell in Status, above, shows the saved raw angle, or “not taught” (boot position = 0°).

Update 操作說明 ↗
  1. Check the file against the board
  2. Upload to slot 1
  3. Mark slot 1 for a test boot
  4. Reset – MCUboot swaps the images
  5. Reconnect
  6. Verify the new image runs and gets confirmed
 
Switch back
After an update, slot 1 still holds the previous image (MCUboot swaps the slots, it doesn't overwrite). This test-boots it, reconnects and waits for it to confirm itself, so the two images trade places – press again to switch forward. Unavailable once slot 1 has been overwritten by another upload.
Single steps
The board refuses to start an upload or to reset while the motor is moving (a G move or an F/R jog); stop it on the Control tab first. Chunk “auto” sizes each request from the board's SMP buffer size.
